## 2. What the app is, and is not

The app measures facial proportions from guided captures, compares them against published population reference ranges, and suggests self-directed habits graded by the strength of the evidence behind them.

**It is not medical, dental, dermatological, psychological or cosmetic-surgical advice.** It does not diagnose anything. Where the app notes that something "is something a professional could assess", that is the entirety of what it says: it does not recommend any treatment, procedure, prescription or provider, it has no financial relationship with any provider, and it does not tell you how much of anything to use. Talk to a licensed professional before acting on anything in those sections.

**The score is not a claim about objective attractiveness.** It is a measured comparison against published norms weighted by evidence strength, expressed as a percentile of a reference distribution, with a stated uncertainty band. About half of what makes a face appealing to any specific person is personal taste that no measurement captures. The app does not compare you against other users.

## 3. Your wellbeing

Appearance preoccupation is common and treatable. If using this app is making you feel worse rather than better, stop, and consider talking to someone. Those limits are part of the product and are not adjustable.
