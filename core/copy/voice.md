# Voice — the short version for anyone writing a string

The full reasoning is in the design bible ("Voice and copy"). This is the checklist.

**Register.** A technician's. States what was measured and what follows from it. Precise, slightly dry, never hyped, never consoling. Native vocabulary with guardrails: harmony, PSL tiers, canthal tilt, midface, softmaxxing — yes. Anything that ranks people against each other — no.

**Hard rules (tests enforce the ones marked ✓):**

- ✓ Every string passes `data/denylist.json`. Fix the string, never the list.
- ✓ No exclamation marks. Anywhere.
- ✓ Never "your score is", never "unlock your".
- ✓ The five evidence badges are exact strings, no variants.
- Percentile is against *the reference distribution*, never against other users. "68th percentile of the reference distribution", never "you beat 68% of guys".
- Low scores are stated flat and move straight to the most improvable finding. No preamble, no encouragement — both read as pity.
- Finding copy pattern: **measurement, reference, plain reading.** No judgement adjective. "Jaw angle slightly more obtuse than the typical range", never "weak jaw".
- Locked state names what is behind the lock: "Gonial angle · measured, locked". The free score is the real score; copy must never imply a better number is hidden.
- Paywall: price, period, renewal terms on the surface. No scarcity, no countdowns, no social proof.
- Tier 3/4 cards end with: "We don't recommend procedures and we have no financial relationship with any provider."
- Drugs may be *named* in tier 3 cards (minoxidil, finasteride, tretinoin) and never dosed. No caloric figures, no goal weights, no deficits. Rate-of-loss guidance only, as a range in words.
- The distress card is calm, unstyled, never A/B tested, never an engagement surface.
- The app name is `{app_name}` in every string. The working name is itself on the denylist.

**Words that look harmless and aren't:** *just* (minimising), *unfortunately*, *weak*, *poor*, *fix* (for bone), *ideal* (use *typical range*), *perfect*, *flaw*.

**Numbers.** Tabular. One decimal on the score (6.4), whole degrees on angles (128°), three significant figures on ratios (0.228). Bands as "likely 5.9–6.9".
