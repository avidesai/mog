# Privacy — settled

- No image, depth, landmark, mesh, embedding or projected point is ever persisted, synced or sent (11 Nov; reaffirmed 12 Nov for overlays).
- Sync is a separate, off-by-default consent. Calibration contribution would be a third consent; not asked for in v1.
- Consent records are retained 3 years after deletion as evidence; everything else cascades within 30 days.
- No advertising or attribution SDK at launch.
- Illinois, Texas, Washington excluded from face measurement at launch.
- The first revenue funds counsel review of the three legal drafts.
