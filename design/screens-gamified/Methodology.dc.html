How the score works

Your score estimates how an average rater would score this capture, as a percentile against a reference set matched to your sex and age. It is not a measure of how attractive you are.

WHAT WE DON'T CLAIM

About half of what makes a face appealing to any one person is their own taste, and no model can measure it. We predict the average, and show the range.

WHERE THE RANGES COME FROM

Published anthropometric norms, split by sex and ancestry where the literature supports it. Anything that comes from community convention instead is labelled as such on the measurement itself.

IF YOUR SCORE CHANGES

Either your measurements changed, or we updated the model. If it's the model, we say so, show both numbers, and explain what moved.
