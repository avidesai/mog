HOW IT WAS MEASURED

The angle where the back edge of the jaw meets the bottom edge, read off your profile capture after it was rotated level. Median of the 8 frames that passed. The ± is how much those frames disagreed.

Profile capture 8 of 10 frames
THE RANGE Reasonable support

115–125° is the typical range for adult males in the published cephalometric norms we use. There is moderate evidence that a sharper angle reads as more defined; it isn't one of the strongest predictors of how a face is rated.

Where the ranges come from
WHAT WOULD MOVE IT

The bone won't. The soft tissue over it will — your facial fullness reading is high, and that's what makes this angle look softer than it measures. Lowering body fat is already in your plan for exactly this reason.

Jaw exercisers and "mewing" don't change this. There's no evidence either moves the bone in adults.
