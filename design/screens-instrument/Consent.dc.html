Before we scan

To measure your face, the app reads its geometry from the camera.

Where it happens

Entirely on this iPhone. Your photos, the face mesh and the landmark positions never leave the device.

What is kept

The photos are deleted as soon as your measurements are computed. The measurements themselves — numbers like "128°" — stay on the device.

For how long

Until you delete them. One tap in Settings erases everything, and nothing is recoverable afterwards.

Who else sees it

Nobody. We don't sell it, share it, or send it to advertisers or analytics.
