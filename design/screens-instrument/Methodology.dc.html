How this works

Your score estimates how an average rater would score this capture, as a percentile against a reference distribution matched to your sex and age band. It is not a measure of how attractive you are.

What we don't claim

Roughly half of what makes a face appealing to any specific person is their own taste, and no model can measure that. Research puts rater agreement high in aggregate and individual preference at about the same size. We predict the average, with a stated range.

Where the ranges come from

Published anthropometric and cephalometric norms, stratified by sex and ancestry where the literature supports it. Anything that comes from community convention rather than published data is labelled as such on the measurement itself.

If your score changes

It changes because your measurements changed, or because we updated the model — and if it's the model, we tell you, show the old number and the new one, and explain what moved.
